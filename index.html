How to play

Travel Earth → Moon → Mars → Moon → Earth. Each question shows a NASA observation. Guess the world to advance your ship. Wrong guesses move you backward and cost extra fuel. Reach a base to refuel. Run out of fuel before landing and the mission fails.

Only Easy is available at first. Finish all four flights to unlock Medium, then finish all four on Medium to unlock Hard. Progress is saved on this device. Difficulty changes fuel supply, forward progress, and navigation penalties. The flight path and fuel usage are simplified arcade mechanics—not NASA flight-dynamics predictions.

Photos link to their original NASA records after you answer. If a NASA image cannot load, a labeled illustration and text clue appear so the game still works.
