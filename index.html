MISSION GUIDE

How to play

Every difficulty follows the same three-flight round trip: Earth → Moon → Mars → Earth. Finish Easy back on Earth to unlock Medium, then complete that loop again on Medium to unlock Hard, and again on Hard to unlock endless Insane. Guess NASA landscapes to advance, earn points and manage fuel. Every flight offers two choices: Earth or Moon, Moon or Mars, or Mars or Earth depending on your route. On Insane, one wrong answer ends your run immediately!

Only Easy is available initially. Return to Earth from Mars on Easy to unlock Medium, on Medium to unlock Hard, and on Hard to unlock Insane. Each difficulty starts on Earth, and the game changes difficulties automatically at each Earth arrival. Correct answers earn 100, 200 and 350 points on Easy, Medium and Hard respectively. Insane starts at 500 points and increases each correct-answer reward by 10% over the last. Difficulty unlocks are saved on this device. Fuel and trajectories are simplified arcade rules.

Photos link to their original NASA records after you answer. If a NASA image cannot load, a labeled illustration and text clue appear so the game still works.
