## Additional engineering and hackathon references

- [Design and Develop Science Missions (NASA)](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/250323-design-and-develop-science-mission.pdf) — **resource from the Space Mission Design Game challenge**: the objective, spacecraft configuration, flight and base operations in this prototype take inspiration from the systems-engineering segments. The game does not replicate the scientific trajectory models.
- [NASA Space Apps 2026](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/) — project challenge / submission context.
- [NASA General Mission Analysis Tool](https://software.nasa.gov/software/GSC-17177-1) — example of real engineering trajectory tools, **not used for calculations in this arcade prototype**.
- [NASA NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov/) — additional NASA imagery access.

## Image processing and honesty

- Some spacecraft images use calibrated or enhanced color, not natural-color human-eye representations. The interface labels enhanced-color entries when available.
- The NASA Photojournal page is the source of the world, title, source instrument and mission.
- The game deliberately does not present a visual question as a physics simulation.
- All 23 entries listed above are referenced by the code.
